# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are HR recruiters and administrators working in an internal applicant-tracking system. They review hiring pipelines on desktop and laptop screens, move deliberately between candidate, job, and account-management tasks, and need an interface that makes consequential actions clear without slowing routine work.

## Product Purpose

The product helps an organization run a defensible recruitment workflow: secure access, job and candidate administration, explainable screening, and structured hiring decisions. Success means recruiters can understand the current state of work quickly, act with confidence, and retain human control over hiring outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Composed, authoritative, approachable. The interface should feel calm enough for careful review, decisive enough for operational work, and polished enough to represent a credible internal platform without becoming cold or bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

- Generic student CRUD screens with undifferentiated cards, arbitrary controls, and weak hierarchy.
- Purple-gradient or decorative glass SaaS aesthetics that compete with operational content.
- Noisy "command center" dashboards that substitute visual density for clarity.
- Color-only status communication or motion that distracts from review and decision-making.

## Design Principles

1. **Calm structure, decisive action.** Keep the information field quiet and legible; reserve visual emphasis for decisions, primary actions, and workflow state.
2. **Trust through operational clarity.** Make ownership, status, constraints, and consequences visible before an HR user commits an action.
3. **Evidence over decoration.** Use color, typography, and layout to clarify real product information rather than to simulate sophistication.
4. **Approachable precision.** Pair enterprise-grade structure with humane language, predictable controls, and comfortably readable density.
5. **Accessible by default.** Every workflow is usable with a keyboard, communicates independently of color, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation and visible focus treatment; preserve 4.5:1 minimum contrast for standard text; provide text or icon labels alongside color-coded states; maintain responsive desktop, laptop, and tablet operation; and provide reduced-motion alternatives for every non-essential animation.
